Reading

Languages in the book

Text recognition runs in this browser (Tesseract). The reader and the dictionaries download from the internet the first time; your pages are not uploaded anywhere. Count on 3 to 10 seconds per scanned page.

Result

The right side shows each page with what was found: headings in blue, pictures and captions in green, quotes and poems in purple, tables in olive, index entries in pink, footnotes in orange, left-out running heads and page numbers grey, uncertain words in red. Click a box to change its kind (it can also be made a table or index entries), drag its handles to resize it, or remove it; drag on the page to add a box. Click a red word, or “Check uncertain words”, to correct words close up. The text below follows every change.

Or save the result

“Use as manuscript” replaces the text on the Manuscript tab and adds the pictures, title, author, colophon, dedication and index names that were found. The saved project opens again here, or in bookmaker.py.

Each heading becomes a chapter unless you give it another role. Front and back sections (preface, sources, acknowledgements) start on a right-hand page and are not numbered.

A KDP paperback or hardcover is made from two files: the inside pages (a PDF without cover) and the cover (one PDF holding back cover, spine and front cover). The steps below make both. A Kindle e-book needs an EPUB and a cover image.
